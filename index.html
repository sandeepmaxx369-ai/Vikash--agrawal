How does the PM Surya Ghar Govt. Subsidy work? +
Under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana & State Government Solar initiatives, residential consumers can receive total subsidies up to ₹1,08,000 directly credited into their bank accounts:

• Central Government Subsidy (PM Surya Ghar): Up to ₹78,000
• State Government Subsidy: Up to ₹30,000
• Total Maximum Subsidy: ₹1,08,000

Vikash Agarwal handles the complete online portal paperwork, DISCOM net-metering approvals, and inspection process so both subsidies are released without any hassle.
What brands of solar panels and inverters do you supply? +
We only supply 100% genuine Tier-1 brands with manufacturer warranty cards. For solar panels, we provide Tata Power Solar, Waaree Energies, and Adani Solar. For solar inverters and battery backup, we deal in Luminous, Havells, and Microtek.
What is Net-Metering and how does it save money? +
A bi-directional net meter records both the electricity you consume from the grid and the excess solar units your rooftop panels export to the grid during the day. Your monthly bill is only calculated on the "net" difference. If you export more than you use, the credits carry forward to offset future bills!
Do solar panels produce power on cloudy or rainy days? +
Yes! Modern high-efficiency Mono PERC and Topcon panels continue to produce 25% to 40% of their peak capacity even in overcast weather by capturing diffused daylight. At night or during extreme storms, your grid connection automatically ensures zero power interruption.
